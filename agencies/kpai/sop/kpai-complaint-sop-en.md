# KPAI complaint service SOP (English translation)

English translation of KPAI's public complaint SOP infographic and its "Alur Aduan KPAI" (complaint flow) carousel (kpai.go.id, @kpai_official). The Indonesian transcription is in sop-pengaduan-kpai.md. Used as service knowledge in the Embrace demo; the official documents belong to KPAI.

## 1. Complaints made in person

Free of charge. In-person complaints: Monday to Thursday 09:00 to 15:00 WIB (break 12:00 to 13:00), Friday 09:00 to 15:00 WIB (break 11:30 to 13:00). Closed on Saturday, Sunday and public holidays.
1. The complainant brings the documents (copy of ID card, the victim's birth certificate, family card, etc.).
2. Front office: the complainant signs the complaints guest book and waits in the queue.
3. Front desk: checks the documents, identifies whether the case concerns child protection, and enters the complaint form into the database.
4. Complaints officer: receives the complaint and assesses it; writes a summons to the reported party for clarification (up to 3 summonses); clarifies with the reported party; hands the case to the assistant or commissioner of the relevant cluster; writes a referral letter if the case needs a service close to the victim.
5. Cluster assistant: writes summonses and schedules mediation (up to 3 summonses); oversees, monitors and reports on cases referred to other agencies.
6. Cluster commissioner: analyses the case (fact finding, home or site visit if needed); mediates and/or writes a referral letter. The commissioner chooses either mediation or referral.
7. KPAI chair: reviews and approves the recommendation and the mediation or referral letter to the agency concerned.

## 2. Registering an in-person complaint

Documents needed: the complainant's identity, the child's identity and supporting information about the complaint.

## 3. Registering an online complaint

Online complaints arrive by letter, email, WhatsApp and social media.
- The complainant is asked to fill in their data through a form link.
- If the data is incomplete, an officer contacts the complainant within 3 x 24 hours.
- If the data is complete, an officer gets back to the complainant within 14 working days.

## 4. Assessment of the complaint by a social worker

Based on the assessment:
1. If the complainant has not yet used any child protection service, they receive psychoeducation and a referral to a service provider.
2. If the complainant has used a child protection service but was not handled as expected, KPAI carries out oversight of that service.
Child protection service providers: SAPA 129, UPTD PPA (local women and child protection units), the social affairs office (Dinas Sosial), the education office and the police.

## 5. Oversight of complaints about violations of children's rights

1. Clarification and examination of the agency being overseen.
2. Mediation if needed.
3. Drafting the oversight recommendation.
4. Delivering the oversight recommendation.

## 6. Monitoring

KPAI monitors ministries, agencies, local governments and law enforcement on how they follow up KPAI's recommendations.

## 7. Closing a case

A case is closed with the complainant's agreement, or unilaterally if the complainant cannot be reached 3 times within 3 weeks.
