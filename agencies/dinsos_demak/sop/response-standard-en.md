# Complaint response standard (Embrace sample for Dinsos P2PA Demak, English)

Sample response standard written for the Embrace demo from the deadlines in PermenPANRB 5/2025 and the service flows of Dinsos P2PA Demak. Not an official Dinsos P2PA Demak document; replace it with the office's own SOP. Indonesian version: standar-respons-pengaduan.md.

## 1. Receiving complaints

Complaints are accepted from every channel: in person (Puskesos, SLRT, the office), letter, phone, WhatsApp, email, suggestion box, social media, SP4N-LAPOR! and Embrace chat. Every complaint gets a ticket number and an acknowledgement. Filing a complaint is free. The complainant's personal data is kept confidential.

## 2. Emergencies

Emergencies: violence happening now, a woman or child thrown out of their home, a family with no food today or nowhere to sleep tonight, victims of floods, tidal floods (rob) or fire who have not evacuated, people with mental illness who are chained (pasung) or neglected, and threats of suicide. Emergency complaints go straight to the duty supervisor and get a response within 2 hours at most. For violence against women and children, the UPTD PPA officer coordinates with the police women and children unit and points to SAPA 129. Late social aid alone is not an emergency.

## 3. Follow-up deadlines

Following PermenPANRB 5/2025 Article 21: 5 working days for information requests and normative complaints; 14 working days for complaints that need no field inspection; 60 working days for complaints that need oversight and a field inspection. If the complainant's data is incomplete, the officer asks for it; complaints not completed within 10 working days are archived.

## 4. Social aid not received (PKH, BPNT/Sembako, BLT)

The Social Protection and Security officer checks within 3 working days: the recipient's status in DTSEN, the payment status for the current round, and the status of the KKS card or bank account. If several residents of one village report the same problem, the officer checks the whole payment round and coordinates with the PKH companion and the e-warong shop.

## 5. Illegal levies and distribution fraud

Aid recipients never have to pay anything. Reports of illegal levies by e-warong agents, group leaders or village officials are recorded without revealing the reporter's identity, checked by the distribution oversight team, and passed to the Inspectorate or law enforcement if proven.

## 6. DTSEN data and certificates

Registration, status checks and data corrections in DTSEN (formerly DTKS) are handled by SLRT or Puskesos; bring your ID card (KTP) and family card (KK). Wrong data, such as a family member who has died, is corrected through the village office. See the SLRT flow.
