# Service flows of the Demak Regency Social Affairs Office (English translation)

English translation of the service-flow infographics published by Dinas Sosial, Pemberdayaan Perempuan dan Perlindungan Anak (Dinsos P2PA) Kabupaten Demak (dinsosp2pa.demakkab.go.id). The Indonesian transcription is in alur-layanan-dinsos-p2pa-demak.md. Used as service knowledge in the Embrace demo; the official documents belong to Dinsos P2PA Demak.

## 1. Puskesos complaint flow

1. A resident comes in or submits a complaint: needs, problems or complaints go to the Puskesos (village social welfare centre) manager in person, by phone or through complaint media.
2. Registration and verification: the officer records the resident's identity and does an initial check of the complaint.
3. Identifying the need: the officer identifies the type of need or problem, such as social aid, health, education, civil registration or other social services.
4. Referral or coordination: if needed, Puskesos coordinates with or refers the resident to the relevant government office, agency or institution.
5. Follow-up: the officer monitors and helps resolve the resident's problem.
6. Completion and records: the result is recorded in the Puskesos administration and the resident is told the outcome.

## 2. SLRT flow: certificate of registration in the DTKS / poverty database

The Integrated Service and Referral System (SLRT) accepts requests in person, by phone, WhatsApp, email or the suggestion box.
1. The service user applies for a certificate of registration in DTKS (now DTSEN) or the poverty database.
2. Data recording and initial verification: the SLRT team records the applicant's data and checks the requirements. General requirements: copy of the ID card (KTP), copy of the family card (KK), the application form, and supporting documents if needed.
3. Coordination with the sub-coordinator, head of division or head of office and the team: administrative check and data check in the DTKS/DTSEN system, with a field visit if needed.
4. The analysis is written up from the data and checks; a follow-up plan form is made if needed.
5. The certificate is issued to the applicant according to the rules.

## 3. SLRT flow: PBI-Pemda / UHC health insurance recommendation

1. A resident applies for a PBI-Pemda / UHC (government-paid health insurance) recommendation through SLRT, in person, by letter, suggestion box, WhatsApp or email.
2. The SLRT team records the complaint or request in the system and the SLRT form.
3. The SLRT team reports the recap to the sub-coordinator, head of division and head of office.
4. Coordination and handling: administrative check and field visit if needed.
5. Eligibility analysis based on the checks and coordination with the parties involved.
6. A follow-up plan form for the PBI-Pemda / UHC recommendation is prepared.
7. The PBI-Pemda / UHC recommendation is issued and given to the applicant.

## 4. SLRT flow: BPJS employment insurance claims for vulnerable workers

1. Complaints, suggestions or input are submitted in writing through the suggestion box, SMS or email.
2. Suggestion box, SMS and email submissions are collected.
3. The team gathers the users' complaints.
4. The head of division instructs the team to carry out administrative and factual checks.
5. The recap is reported and coordinated with the manager.
6. The team records the complaints, suggestions and input.
7. The results of the checks are analysed.
8. The head of division and the team agree a follow-up plan (old-age savings, work accident and death benefits for vulnerable workers).

## 5. Handling violence against women and children

1. Report by the service user: in person, by letter, phone or complaint media.
2. First response in parallel: coordination and first support with the police women and children unit (Unit PPA Polres); counselling and first psychosocial support; a home visit if needed to assess the victim.
3. Medical examination (visum) at a health facility.
4. Problem analysis: needs and a handling plan.
5. Psychological support: further counselling and recovery support.
6. Legal aid: support through the legal process and access to related services.
If the victim is in danger now, call 110 (police) or SAPA 129 (WhatsApp 08111-129-129).

## 6. Aid for victims of natural and social disasters

1. Start.
2. The request for aid is submitted to the Regent through Dinas Sosial P2PA; the request is received and processed.
3. Site visit to the disaster location.
4. Assessment of the site visit.
5. Identifying the recipients' needs and the type of aid.
6. Deciding the amount of aid for the victims.
7. Approving the amount; if not approved, it is discussed again.
8. Preparing the relief goods according to need.
9. Distributing the aid to the victims according to need.
10. Documenting the handover as evidence.
11. Recording the time, place, type and amount of aid distributed.
12. End.

## 7. Social rehabilitation service for people with disabilities

1. Complaint from or about a person with a disability: in person, by letter, phone or complaint media.
2. The officer receives the complaint: recording and initial verification.
3. The officer carries out an assessment of the person's needs and condition. Possible services: health (examination and medical referral), psychological (counselling), legal (legal aid and support), social (social support and empowerment), economic (training, access to work, economic aid).
4. Assessment result: discussion and a service plan; family reunification support where the family needs strengthening.
5. Referral to the relevant service or institution.
6. Completion: monitoring and follow-up.

## 8. Referring homeless people and beggars to a social rehabilitation centre

1. The officer receives homeless people, beggars or neglected people from the municipal police (Satpol PP), the police or other agencies.
2. The officer identifies and records them; the case is rejected or accepted.
3. The officer gives counselling.
4. The officer prepares an intervention plan.
5. The officer carries out the intervention (guidance).
6. The officer evaluates, closes and refers: back to the family or to a social rehabilitation centre (Balai Rehabsos).
7. The officer gives follow-up guidance through home visits.

## 9. Admission to the shelter (rumah singgah)

1. The officer receives a report from the public; rejected if the criteria are not met, accepted if they are.
2. The officer identifies and selects prospective beneficiaries.
3. The officer assesses the beneficiary (interview, observation and needs review).
4. The officer prepares an intervention plan.
5. The officer carries out the intervention (support, basic needs, mental and social strengthening).
6. The officer evaluates, closes and refers: back to the family (reunification) or to a social rehabilitation centre.
7. The officer gives follow-up guidance through home visits to make sure the service continues and the beneficiary recovers.

## 10. Recommendation on child marriage (early marriage)

1. The applicant requests a recommendation on an early (child) marriage and completes the requirements.
2. The administrative officer receives and checks the documents.
3. Coordination with the head of the child marriage prevention working group (POKJA KHPK).
4. A service officer (social worker or psychologist) provides service, assessment and support to the applicant.
5. Eligibility analysis: the assessment, social and psychological conditions and related aspects.
6. If found eligible, a recommendation letter is issued.
7. The letter is handed to the applicant for use according to the rules.

## 11. Child adoption

1. Prospective adoptive parents come to the social rehabilitation division and hand in the required documents.
2. The officer checks the documents; incomplete or ineligible files are returned with an explanation.
3. The officer makes a home visit for initial verification and assessment.
4. The officer prepares the adoption approval letter.
5. The officer makes a second verification home visit.
6. The officer issues the adoption recommendation letter.

## 12. Renewing the registration of a care home or social welfare institution (LKS)

1. The officer receives the renewal request with the requirements.
2. The officer checks that the documents are complete and correct.
3. The officer submits the registration recommendation (SIOP), which is handed to the applicant.

## 13. Recommendation for cleft lip surgery

1. The person needing the service comes in, fills in the guest book and complaint book and explains the complaint.
2. The person hands in the required documents; incomplete applications are rejected with an explanation.
3. The officer refers the person to a hospital that treats cleft lip.
4. The officer prepares an intervention plan.
5. The officer provides counselling as needed.
